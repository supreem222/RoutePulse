Why RoutePulse Transforms Transit in Nepal

Problems Before RoutePulse

  • Uncertain Wait Times: Passengers wait for hours without knowing when local vehicles arrive.
  • Manual Seat Booking: Double-booking of seats and price inflation at local counters.
  • Rural Isolation: Villages and remote districts are excluded from online booking systems.

Solutions by RoutePulse

  • Live GPS Telematics: Tracks vehicles on OpenStreetMap with precise station ETAs.
  • Universal Network: Integrates major highways and rural village Jeep routes into one engine.
  • Flexible Booking & Payments: Instant seat reservation supporting Digital Wallets and Cash.
